The difference

Every other visual tool plays clips. This one plays the room.

VJ software gives you a media library and expects somebody to stand behind a laptop operating it. Music visualisers draw generative shapes that have nothing to do with anyone present. Make Party Alive points a camera at your actual crowd and turns them into the artwork — which is why people film it.

The crowd is the content

Face-aware looks respond to the people in frame. Shroom World spawns a character for every person it sees, so the room populates the scene.

02

It runs itself

Autopilot changes the look on musical phrase boundaries and slams a change on the drop. Beat FX rotate on their own. Nobody has to watch it.

No extra hardware

Beat sync

It reacts to the music, not just to loudness.

Onsets are detected per frequency band against a rolling average, and each hit is scored on how sharp it is as well as how loud — so a breakdown kick reads softer than a drop kick. BPM comes from the median of recent kick intervals, and drops are found from bass presence and energy surge rather than raw volume, which is why a loud filtered build doesn't fool it.

Questions

Before you download.

Windows says the file isn't safe. Is something wrong?

No. The app isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about it the way it warns about any new independent release. Choose More info then Run anyway. Signing is on the roadmap.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs locally, including licence checks. Venues rarely have usable wifi, so it was built not to need any.

Which DJ software does it listen to?

rekordbox is the confirmed path — it captures the app's audio directly, with no cables. Serato, VirtualDJ and Traktor work through the same app capture or through your output device. Anything audible can drive it, including a mic pointed at the room.

Do I need a powerful graphics card?

Not for the eighteen live effects, which run on the processor. The four painted styles use the GPU and want a DirectX 12 card to stay smooth.

Do I need to be a DJ to use this?

No. It listens to whatever is playing — rekordbox, Spotify, a phone through the speakers, or just the room itself through a mic. If there's music and a wall, it works.

What's the catch with the free version?

Only the watermark across the output. Every look, Autopilot and the beat sync are all included, with no time limit and no account. The $20 licence removes the watermark, once, permanently.
